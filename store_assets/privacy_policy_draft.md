# Prismleaf Vale privacy policy draft

**Publication status:** Draft. Add the publisher name, contact address, effective date, and a stable public URL before submission. Review the final app and Google SDK configuration before publishing this text.

Prismleaf Vale is an Android match-three game. It does not require an account or a game server. The app stores unlocked levels, scores, daily reward dates and streaks, glow balance, and sound and color preferences in local app storage. This progress is removed when app storage is cleared or the app is uninstalled, unless the device platform restores app data from a backup.

The app can show a banner on the level map and an optional rewarded ad after a failed level. Playing the core game does not require watching an ad. Ad requests happen only after the Google User Messaging Platform consent flow has been checked and the SDK reports that ads can be requested. Depending on region and user choices, Google Mobile Ads may process IP address, app interactions, diagnostics, and device or account identifiers for ad delivery, measurement, and fraud prevention. Google describes its SDK's data practices at https://developers.google.com/admob/android/privacy/play-data-disclosure . Google's privacy policy is at https://policies.google.com/privacy .

Where required, the Settings screen provides a Privacy options entry for changing ad choices. The app itself does not sell local gameplay progress or upload it to a game backend.
